# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## 24-hour risk-free cancellation

Any booking made through Wanderlux may be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours
of purchase, provided the travel date is at least 7 days away at the time of
booking. The full amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5
business days. This window applies regardless of fare class.

## Refund eligibility by fare class

Flexible fares are fully refundable at any time before departure, minus a $0
processing fee. Standard fares are refundable up to 72 hours before departure,
minus a $75 processing fee. Basic fares are non-refundable outside the 24-hour
risk-free window; the client may request a future travel credit instead, valid for
12 months, at the agency's discretion.

## Refunds for agency error

If a booking error is attributable to Wanderlux — a duplicate charge, a booking
made against the client's stated dates, or a fare quoted incorrectly by an agent —
the full amount is refunded regardless of fare class, and no processing fee
applies. Agent error should be noted explicitly in the proposed action so the
manager can confirm it before approving.

## Refunds involving denied boarding or cancellation by the carrier

When a flight is cancelled or the client is denied boarding by the airline itself
(not by the client's own choice), the client is entitled to a full refund or a
rebooking on the next available flight at no additional cost, per the carrier's
own contract of carriage. These cases should be escalated rather than resolved by
a front-line agent alone, since compensation amounts vary by carrier and route.

## Amounts requiring manager sign-off

Any refund or credit over $1,500 requires a manager's approval regardless of which
of the above categories it falls under. This is a control on total exposure, not a
judgment about whether the refund is warranted.
