# Rebooking and Change Fee Policy

## Standard change fees

Changing the date or destination of an existing booking incurs a change fee set by
fare class: $0 for flexible fares, $100 for standard fares, and $250 for basic
fares, plus any fare difference between the original and new itinerary. The fee is
waived automatically within the 24-hour risk-free window described in the refund
policy.

## Fee waivers for documented hardship

A change fee may be waived, independent of fare class, when the client provides
a reasonable account of one of the following: a medical emergency affecting the
traveler or an immediate family member, a bereavement requiring travel to a
funeral, or a natural disaster or government travel advisory affecting the
destination. Waiving the fee does not require documentation to be attached before
the proposal is created, but the manager approving it should note the stated
reason in the decision.

## Same-day changes

A change requested for travel departing within 24 hours should always be flagged
for immediate manager attention rather than queued normally — same-day fare
availability changes quickly, and a delay in review can mean the original problem
is no longer solvable by the time it is seen.

## Multi-passenger bookings

When a booking includes more than one traveler, a change fee applies once per
booking, not per traveler, unless travelers are being split into separate
itineraries — in which case each new itinerary is treated as its own booking for
fee purposes.

## What agents may not do

A front-line agent may propose a fee waiver but may not confirm one to the client
before a manager has approved it. Quoting a specific waived amount to a client
ahead of approval creates an expectation the agency may not be able to honor.
